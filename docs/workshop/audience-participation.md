# Audience participation

Tool: **Slido**, one event for this session. Q&A is open all hour, plus
exactly three polls, which is the free-plan poll budget.

## Free-plan limits

The orchestrator checked Slido's free **Basic** plan against official docs
on 8 November 2026: **100 participants per event**, **3 polls plus 1 quiz
per event** (word clouds and ratings each count as a poll), and **unlimited
Q&A**. If you need more polls, create a second event. If the room plus
guests could exceed 100, use a second event or the fallbacks below.

## The interactions

| # | When | Slide | Type | Exact question | Options | How the result feeds the talk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q&A | All hour | 3 (announce), 16 (use) | Q&A with upvotes | — | — | Slide 16 starts with the top-voted questions. |
| 1 | 0:03 | 3 | Word cloud, up to 3 entries per person | **If you could hire a team of software developers to build you a digital tool (dashboard, website, app, experience, etc.), what would you build?** | Open text | Read the top answers now. On slide 13 they become candidate tools #2–#n for the Agentic Marketing Toolkit. After class, turn the top ones into issues. |
| 2 | 0:23 | 9 | Multiple choice, single answer | **Which industry should Five Forces add, right now?** | A. Ski resorts · B. Craft breweries · C. Food trucks | The winner goes into the agent prompt on slide 10. Each option has a fallback branch. |
| 3 | 0:26 | 11 | Rating 1–5 | **AI coding agents: how high is the threat of new entrants? (1 = very low, 5 = very high)** | 1 Very low · 2 Low · 3 Moderate · 4 High · 5 Very high | The most common answer goes into Five Forces as the rating for a custom industry, "AI coding agents". Ask people at the extremes to defend it. The app labels it "My judgment / assumption". |

Set up all three tonight, in order and inactive, and activate each one from
the presenter tab. Poll 3 uses the app's own scale: a higher rating always
means more pressure on industry profitability.

## Fallback (Slido or Wi-Fi fails)

- **Poll 1:** call out five tool ideas by show of hands. Write them down
  for slide 13.
- **Poll 2:** show of hands for A, B, C. Tie: Bill picks.
- **Poll 3:** fist-to-five (hold up 1–5 fingers), and take the most common
  number by eye.
- **Q&A:** hands only.
