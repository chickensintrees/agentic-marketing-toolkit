# Audience participation

The session uses one Slido event. It has **one poll**, asked after
everything has been explained, and **Q&A**, which is open all session and
used at the end.

## Free-plan limits

The orchestrator checked Slido's free **Basic** plan against the official
docs on 8 November 2026. It allows 100 participants per event, 3 polls plus
1 quiz per event, and unlimited Q&A. One poll is well within these limits.

## The interactions

| | When | Slide | Type | Exact wording | How it is used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poll | 0:41 | 14 | Word cloud, up to 3 entries per person | **If you could hire a team of software developers to build you a digital tool (dashboard, website, app, experience, etc.), what would you have them build?** | Read the most common answers as candidate tools for the toolkit. Slide 15 then shows how to start one. After class, turn the top answers into issues labeled as new-tool ideas. |
| Q&A | Open all session; used at 0:50 | 18 | Q&A with upvotes | — | Take the top-voted questions first, then hands. |

The industry for the live demonstration is not polled. Bill chooses it, or
takes one suggestion from the room (slide 11, `[INDUSTRY]`).

Set up the poll tonight, inactive, and activate it from the presenter tab
on slide 14. Turn on Q&A from the start.

## Fallback (Slido or Wi-Fi fails)

- **Poll:** take five tool ideas by show of hands, and write them down.
- **Q&A:** hands only.
