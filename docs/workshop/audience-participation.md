# Audience participation

Tool: **Slido**, one event for this session. Q&A is open all hour, plus
three polls. That is exactly the free-plan poll budget (see "Limits").

## Free-plan limits (check before relying on them)

Slido's free **Basic** plan is reported as allowing **up to 100 participants
per event**, **3 polls per event** (a word cloud counts as a poll), one quiz,
and unlimited Q&A. Sources: Slido's community forum and third-party reviews.
One review site says some regions get five polls, and one article says the
participant cap may be counted per device. **Tonight, confirm the current
numbers at slido.com/pricing and in the event's own settings.** If the class
plus guests could exceed 100, or polls are capped below 3, use the fallback
below or a paid/edu plan.

## The interactions

| # | When | Slide | Type | Exact question | Options | How the result feeds the talk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q&A | All hour | 3 (announce), 14 (use) | Q&A with upvotes | — | — | Slide 14 starts with the top-voted questions. |
| 1 | 0:03 | 3 | Word cloud | **One word: how do you feel about AI building software?** | Open text, one word | Read three or four words aloud now. At the close (slide 15), show it again and ask "Did your word change?" |
| 2 | 0:20 | 8 | Multiple choice, single answer | **Which industry should the app add, right now?** | A. Ski resorts · B. Craft breweries · C. Food trucks | The winner goes into the agent prompt on slide 9. Each option has a fallback branch. |
| 3 | 0:25 | 10 | Rating 1–5 (or multiple choice 1–5) | **AI coding agents: how high is the threat of new entrants? (1 = very low, 5 = very high)** | 1 Very low · 2 Low · 3 Moderate · 4 High · 5 Very high | The most common answer goes into the app as the rating for "Threat of new entrants" on the custom industry "AI coding agents". Ask people at the extremes to defend their rating. The app labels it "My judgment / assumption", which sets up slide 12. |

Set up polls 1–3 tonight, in that order, all inactive. Activate each one
live from the presenter tab.

**Wording note for poll 3:** use the app's own scale and direction. In the
app, a higher rating always means more pressure on industry profitability
(`SPEC.md` §2.3).

## Fallback (Slido or Wi-Fi fails)

- **Poll 1:** show of hands: excited, worried, both. Skip the callback at the
  close.
- **Poll 2:** show of hands for A, B, C. Tie: Bill picks.
- **Poll 3:** fist-to-five (hold up 1–5 fingers), and take the most common
  number by eye.
- **Q&A:** hands only.

**Alternative tool:** if Slido's limits don't fit, Mentimeter's free plan is
the usual substitute, but check its current limits the same way. Don't
switch tools on the morning of the talk.
