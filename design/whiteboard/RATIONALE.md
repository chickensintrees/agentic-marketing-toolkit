# Whiteboard: design rationale

**Idea:** the professor's whiteboard sketch of Porter's diagram *is* the app. You tap a box on the drawing, and the pressure shows as marker weight.

- **Typography:** Kalam (bold) is the handwriting for everything drawn on the board: force names, tallies, numerals, nav. Atkinson Hyperlegible is the clean text face for the one-line question and driver labels, chosen for legibility from the back of a lecture hall.
- **Palette** (four whiteboard markers on a board): board `#fbfbf8`, black marker `#1c1d21`, red marker `#b3261e` (strong pressure, 4–5), blue marker `#1f4a8a` (your selection and links), green marker `#1d6b3c` (checked drivers), faded marker `#5d6066` (weak pressure, 1–2, and secondary text). Every ink passes 4.5:1 on the board.
- **Intensity without color:** each force has a tally (|||| = 4, a crossed gate = 5) and a numeral. Arrow stroke width grows with the rating, and strong forces get a second or third pass of the marker. Rivalry gets one scribbled loop per rating point, plus clash marks. Red only repeats what the weight already says.
- **How the visuals are made:** everything is drawn by about 60 lines of inline JS, a tiny "rough marker" engine. It uses seeded jittered quadratic lines, Catmull-Rom loops that overshoot their start the way a hand does, and an SVG `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` filter for marker wobble. Text is never filtered. Seeds are stable, so lines don't jump between renders. Industry doodles (plane, cup, TV, can, raft) and the "next up" tool doodles (SWOT grid, target, persona, 4Ps, value-prop circle+square, journey line, perceptual axes, PESTEL, break-even cross, CLV stairs) use the same engine. Faint "ghosting" of old marker sits in the background.
- **Selection** is a blue loop drawn around the box (animated stroke, off with reduced motion). The notes column holds only the core question, a 1–5 tally picker, and the preset's checked drivers. "+ N more drivers" and "why a 4?" (the first sentence of the preset rationale) appear on demand.
- **Unbuilt tools** on the home shelf are dashed pencil sketches on a whiteboard tray ledge, so they read as "not built yet" without badges.
- **Why it isn't SaaS:** there are no cards, shadows, gradients, pills, CTA buttons, hero or eyebrows. The only "button" (Copy as Markdown) is a hand-boxed word. The look comes from the classroom's own instrument, the marker, and the drawing is the interface.
- **Flow:** Home → one board (pick industry, tap force, rate, tick drivers) → Summary (forces ranked as hatched marker bars, the strongest circled in red, Markdown export). Routes are `#`, `#board` and `#summary`.
- **Accessibility:** forces are keyboard buttons with an aria-label of name, rating and word; rating and industry pickers are radio groups with arrow keys; focus shows a dashed blue marker box. It works at 390 px (picker scrolls sideways).
- **Image prompts:** none needed. If Bill wants a projector title slide: "Overhead photo of a clean classroom whiteboard, Porter's five forces hand-drawn in black and red dry-erase marker, boxes and arrows, slight marker ghosting, soft even daylight, no people, no logos, 16:9."
